YOUR CONTEXT / YOUR CALL

Keep your context.
Share selected memories.

Your agents need common ground. Your team needs the right slice of it. Personal and team workspaces give each a place.

01 / Personal

Start with your own workspace.

Connect your agents to your personal workspace for your notes, preferences, and project context. A separate team workspace does not get access to everything there.

02 / Selective

Choose what crosses over.

Describe the context you want to share, then review and approve exact excerpts for a team or a restricted agent connection.

03 / Together

Give the team a useful copy.

Your teammates and their connected agents can use the shared context. Current memberships and the team’s sharing policy control what is accepted.

Open Sharing in your personal workspace to choose the recipient and review excerpts. New or changed memories need fresh approval. See the sharing model and setup. Everyone in a team workspace can read its shared memory.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
connect the dots.

Looking for setup help?
Start with the support guide.

What exactly do my agents share?

The memory pages and activity in the workspace they are authorized to use. Save useful context through Engram, then recall it from another connected agent. A separate workspace has separate memory storage and scoped access.

Does Engram automatically record everything?

No. It observes requests to its own memory tools. External actions appear when an agent or integration reports them, and those reports remain labeled unverified. A connection alone does not capture every tool call or conversation.

Do I have to change the agent I use?

Keep your existing MCP-compatible client. Guided setup supports Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code, and Windsurf. Chat clients that need OAuth use their connector sign-in flow. See the connection options.

How do I get an invitation?

Hosted Engram is in closed beta. Ask for an invite on X. Open the single-use invite link and verify your chosen inbox; an email-bound invitation needs its invited address. New friend invitations include 60 days of unlimited knowledge transfers after verification. Jev is separate. Existing members sign in with email.

What lives on this shared drive?

Engram holds memory pages and project context: notes, decisions, and the useful details your agents save through MCP. Task activity appears alongside that memory. The shared-drive analogy describes a common place for agent context; it does not promise general file upload or automatic synchronization of every app.

Can I share only part of my context with a team?

Yes. In Sharing, describe the scope, review the proposed excerpts, and explicitly approve what a team or isolated agent connection can read. Suggestions can be wrong; review the actual text. The team’s permissions and sharing policy apply. See the model and current setup path.

Can I run it on my own machine?

Yes. The MIT-licensed app includes the MCP gateway, memory, activity history, and web console. Docker Compose is the local starting point. You operate the infrastructure and any optional providers. Read the self-hosting guide.
