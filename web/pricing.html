A few things to know

What am I paying for?

Cloud is $19 USD per workspace per month at launch. It covers hosting the core Engram app, shared memory, activity history, and agent connections. Taxes may apply. Optional external AI services depend on the operator’s configuration and your workspace’s consent.

How does the trial work?

Each new hosted workspace gets 14 days to try the app without a card. When the trial ends, existing data stays readable and exportable. Subscribe to keep adding or changing content. Checkout starts a paid subscription; it does not extend the free trial.

Is self-hosting really free?

Yes. The source is MIT licensed, and a self-hosted install does not call Engram’s billing service or require an Engram licence key. You pay for your own infrastructure and any optional providers you choose.

What can Engram see?

Engram observes calls to its own memory tools. Other agent actions appear when an agent or integration reports them. Connecting Engram alone does not capture every tool an agent uses.

Is my activity public?

Your workspace is private by default. Only a workspace owner can publish an activity profile. The profile shares broad totals and categories, with no agent names, memory text, resource paths, or transcripts.

Can I cancel?

Yes. A workspace owner can manage or cancel the subscription through Plan & billing. A cancellation at the end of a billing period keeps access until that date. You can still read and export existing data after write access ends. Cancellation does not erase the workspace; request deletion separately.

How do I get help?

Email ani@odstdio.com for account, connection, or billing help. Hosted Engram is operated by Outcome Driven Holdings LLC. We aim to acknowledge requests within two business days. Include your account email and workspace name, without passwords or access tokens.
