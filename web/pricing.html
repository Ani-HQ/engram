PRICING QUESTIONS

Know what
you’re getting.

Prices and availability above follow this installation’s current plan catalog.

What does hosted access include?

The hosted core app includes shared memory, activity history, and agent connections. Current access and pricing appear above. Optional external AI depends on operator configuration and workspace consent.

How does the closed beta work?

Open a single-use invite link, enter your email, and verify that inbox to claim it. An invitation can also be restricted to one email address. New friend invitations include 60 days of unlimited knowledge transfers in a personal workspace, starting after verification, without a card. Your workspace shows the exact end date and a link to ask for an extension. Jev has separate permission and usage costs. Existing paid subscriptions keep their own terms.

What do I pay for with self-hosting?

The Engram source is free under MIT. You cover your infrastructure, backups, operations, and any optional provider costs. Self-hosted mode does not call Engram’s billing service or require a licence key.

How is optional Jev usage billed?

Jev uses TypeSafe for optional routing, ranking, and review suggestions. Core memory, keyword search, and activity work with it off. Jev has its own workspace permission, separate from Voyage embeddings and OpenAI briefs.

Billable Jev requires a paid Cloud subscription, a published customer rate, and the owner’s explicit acceptance. Current rates are loaded from this installation’s plan catalog. Complimentary access alone does not authorize usage charges.

The owner chooses a monthly soft budget. New calls stop when settled spend reaches it; calls already in flight can complete and take the total over budget. Pending calls are not included in settled spend, and unknown usage is excluded from customer billing. A workspace explicitly funded by the operator receives no Jev usage charge.

Are my memories public on any plan?

No. Workspaces are private by default. An owner can choose to publish an aggregate activity profile, which excludes memory contents and detailed private actions. See the data and privacy notice.

What happens when paid write access ends?

Existing data remains readable and exportable. Credential revocation, privacy controls, and unpublishing remain available. Canceling a subscription does not erase your workspace; request erasure separately.

Can I cancel a subscription?

A workspace owner can manage an existing subscription in Plan & billing. Cancellation at the end of the billing period keeps access through that date. Complimentary beta access and an existing paid subscription are separate; a grant does not automatically cancel a subscription.

Who do I contact about billing?

Email ani@odstdio.com with your account email and workspace name. Keep tokens, passwords, payment details, and private memory out of your message.
