Plain, quiet, confident.

One sans typeface at normal spacing, generous whitespace, few colors and no decoration. These are the shared tokens and primitives behind every Engram surface.

Do not use in the app

  • Tags: badges, pills, chips or stickers
  • Italic or accent-colored highlighted words
  • Serif display type or negative letter-spacing
  • Over-detailed, decorative interface
  • Dividers and rule lines
  • Extra color shades, tints and opacities
  • Containers with borders
  • Diagrams
  • Code breadcrumbs and monospace labels
  • Section numbering
